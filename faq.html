QUESTIONS AND ANSWERS


Here you can find answers to the most frequently asked questions. If you do not find the answer to your question, you can write to us or contact us using the details provided on other pages.

How do I book a tour?

You can book a tour directly on our website by selecting the desired date and filling out the booking form.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept credit/debit cards, PayPal, and bank transfers.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel up to 48 hours before the tour for a full refund.

Do you offer private tours?

Yes, we offer private tours. Contact us for more details and pricing.

Is transportation included in the tours?

It depends on the tour. Some include transportation, while others require you to meet at a designated location.
